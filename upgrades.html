Upgrade Services & Scope

Hartup Construction delivers practical, high-quality upgrades that modernise your home while respecting its existing character and structure.

  • Kitchen and bathroom renovations tailored to your layout and budget
  • Open-plan living conversions and internal layout improvements
  • Extensions, decking, and outdoor living upgrades
