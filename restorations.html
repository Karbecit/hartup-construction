Our Approach

Restoration Services & Scope

Hartup Construction approaches every restoration with respect for the original fabric of the building — combining traditional craftsmanship with modern building standards.

  • Sympathetic restoration that respects original architectural features
  • Structural repairs, repointing, and weatherproofing where required
  • Kitchen and bathroom updates that complement period character
  • Timber, brick, and heritage material repair and replacement
  • Compliance upgrades to meet current building standards
  • Detailed documentation and staged works for complex projects
Benefits

Why Restore with Hartup

Restoration is an investment in both history and liveability — delivering a home that feels authentic while working for modern life.

  • Preserve heritage value and street appeal
  • Improve comfort, safety, and energy performance
  • Resolve structural issues before they escalate
  • Blend traditional character with modern functionality
  • Increase long-term property value and marketability
  • Experienced team familiar with restoration challenges
  • Coordinated trades under a single project manager
  • Quality workmanship backed by warranty
Craftsmanship

Quality Restoration Work

From careful preparation through to final finishes, our restoration work prioritises durability, authenticity, and attention to detail.

  • Careful strip-out and protection of retained features
  • Matching profiles, mouldings, and period-appropriate materials
  • Skilled joinery, tiling, and finishing trades
  • Final detailing that honours the original build

Whether you own a heritage-listed property or a character home needing renewal, we assess the building, recommend a practical scope, and deliver restoration work you can trust.
