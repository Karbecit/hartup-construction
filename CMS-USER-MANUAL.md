# Hartup Construction — Website User Manual

A plain-language guide for updating the Hartup Construction website. You do not need technical knowledge to use this.

Open the same guide any time while logged in: **Help** in the top bar, or **User manual** on the Dashboard.

**Admin address:** [https://hartupconstruction.com.au/admin/login.php](https://hartupconstruction.com.au/admin/login.php)

---

## 1. Sign in

1. Open the admin address above.
2. Enter your **username** and **password**.
3. Click **Sign in**.

You will land on the **Dashboard**. From there you can open Pages, Services, Themes, Settings, this manual, or the public website.

**If you forget your password**

1. On the login screen, click **Forgot password?**
2. The next screen shows the **Send enquiries to** address (the same inbox as website contact form messages). A reset link will be sent there.
3. Click **Send reset link**.
4. Open that email (check junk mail too). The link expires after one hour.
5. Choose a new password (at least 10 characters) and confirm it.

---

## 2. Finding your way around

The dark cream bar at the top is always there once you are signed in:

| Button | What it does |
|--------|----------------|
| **Hartup Admin** or **Dashboard** | Home screen of the admin |
| **Pages** | Home, About, Contact, Terms |
| **Services** | New Builds, Kitchens, Bathrooms, and other service pages |
| **Themes** | Colours and fonts (use with care) |
| **Settings** | Login, enquiry email, and technical email settings |
| **View site** | Opens the public website in a new tab |
| **Help** | This user manual |
| **Log out** | Signs you out |

---

## 3. Saving and seeing your changes

- The **Save changes** button (top right on an editor) stays grey until you change something. Click it when you are ready. Wait until it says the page is saved.
- **Save as you go.** If you leave the page without saving, those edits are lost.
- Saving stores your work in the CMS. The **public website** is updated when the site is next **published**. If **View site** still shows old wording, your save is stored but not live yet — ask your web contact (KarBec) to publish.

---

## 4. Pages (Home, About, Contact, Terms)

1. Click **Pages**.
2. Click the page you want (for example **Contact Us**).
3. Edit the header and sections (see below).
4. Click **Save changes**.

**Home** has a large hero: a **tagline** and a **background image**. You can add extra photos so the hero cycles through a slideshow.

**About, Contact, and Terms** have a smaller header: **Eyebrow** (short line above the title), **Heading**, and **Lead** (the intro paragraph).

On those pages you can also:

- **Show in main menu** — include the page in the website menu.
- **Enable page on site** — turn the page on or off.

**Terms and Conditions** can include a downloadable PDF. Use **Upload PDF** (or **Clear** to remove it). Visitors only see the download button after the site is published with that file.

---

## 5. Editing sections

Every page is built from **sections** stacked down the page.

**To add a section**

1. Under **Add a section**, choose a type from the list.
2. Click **Add section**.
3. Fill in the text and images.
4. Click **Save changes**.

**To rearrange**

- Drag the **⋮⋮** handle on a section to move it up or down.

**To hide the details while you work**

- Click the ▸ / ▾ arrow to collapse or expand a section.

**To remove a section**

- Use the remove / delete control on that section, then save.

### Section types

| Type | Use it for |
|------|------------|
| **Image & text** | A photo beside a heading, paragraphs, and optional bullet list. You can put the image on the left or right. |
| **Text only** | Heading and body with no image. |
| **Image only (full width)** | A wide photo. You can add **overlay text** and drag it into place on the picture. |
| **Services / category tiles** | A row of cards that link to service pages (for example New Builds, Kitchens). |
| **Custom layout** | Boxes you can drag and resize. Click a box to edit it. |
| **Design showcase** | House designs with photos, sizes, price-from, floor plan, and optional video. Used on New Builds. |

## 6. Photos

1. Click **Choose image**.
2. Either **upload a new image** or pick one already in the library.
3. If a crop window opens, drag the photo and zoom so the important part sits in the frame. The shaded area is hidden on the website. Confirm when you are happy.

Tips:

- Use a clear, well-lit photo. Very large files are fine — the system compresses them.
- After you change an image, click **Save changes**.
- On full-width images, type overlay text if you want wording on the photo, then drag the text box.

---

## 7. Services (New Builds, Kitchens, and so on)

1. Click **Services**.
2. Click the service you want to edit.

**Service details** (top of the editor)

- **Title** — name of the page.
- **Nav label** — shorter name in the menu, if you want one.
- **URL path** — the web address (for example `/kitchens`). Change this only if you know the old link should move.
- **Tagline** and **Description** — short intro text.
- **Show in main menu** — appear in the top menu.
- **Page visible on site** — hide the page without deleting it.
- **Menu parent** — nest this page under another (for example bedroom layouts under New Builds). Choose **Top level** for a main menu item.

Then add and edit **sections** the same way as Pages.

**To add a new service**

1. On the Services screen, type a title under **Add service / category**.
2. Click **Add**.
3. Fill in details and sections, then save.

### Menu order

On the Services screen, **Main menu order** lists top-level items. Drag the **⋮⋮** handle to change left-to-right order in the website menu. Sub-pages (such as bedrooms under New Builds) stay under their parent.

## 8. House designs (Design showcase)

On a New Builds page (or any page with a **Design showcase** section):

1. Expand the design you want, or click **Add design**.
2. Enter the name, description, size, bedrooms, bathrooms, and price-from.
3. Choose images (hero / floor plan and extra photos). Crop if asked.
4. Optionally add a **floor plan PDF** and a **video** link.
5. Click **Save** on that design if you see a per-design save button, and click **Save changes** for the whole page.

You can collapse designs you are not working on so the list stays tidy.

---

## 9. Themes (colours and fonts)

**Themes** lets you change colours and fonts by clicking parts of a preview.

- **Original** is the Hartup look. It is protected — you cannot overwrite it.
- Click something in the preview, then adjust colour or font on the left.
- **Save As** stores a copy with a name you choose.
- **Apply to site** makes that theme the active one.

If you are unsure, leave Themes alone or ask your web contact before applying a new look.

---

## 10. Settings you might use

Open **Settings**. Each block has its own **Save** button — you do not save the whole page at once.

**Admin login**

- Change your **username** or **password** (leave the password box blank to keep the current one). New passwords must be at least 10 characters.
- Click **Save login**.

**Site**

- **Site URL** is the public website address. Click **Save site**.

**Website enquiries**

- **Send enquiries to** is where contact form messages **and** forgot-password emails go.
- **From name** is the name shown on those emails.
- Click **Save enquiries**.
- If enquiries stop arriving, check junk mail first. If they still do not arrive, ask your web contact.

**Email delivery (SMTP)** and **Spam protection**

- These are locked on purpose. Red warning text reminds you not to change them unless you are sure.
- If you have been asked to edit them, click **Enable editing**, make the change, then click that section’s Save button.
- The wrong values will stop enquiry emails, password resets, or the contact form.

Do not click **Enable editing** on SMTP or spam protection unless your web contact has asked you to.

---

## 11. Website enquiries

Visitors send messages from the **Contact** page. Those emails go to **Send enquiries to** in Settings.

You cannot read old enquiries inside the admin. Check the office inbox (and junk folder).

---

## 12. Quick troubleshooting

| Problem | What to try |
|---------|-------------|
| Cannot sign in | Check caps lock. Use **Forgot password?** After several failed attempts you may need to wait 15 minutes. |
| Save button will not click | Make a change first. If it stays grey, refresh the page — unsaved work will be lost. |
| “Security token expired” | Refresh the page, then try again. |
| Photo looks cropped wrongly | Choose the image again and drag / zoom in the crop window. |
| View site looks unchanged | Save again, then ask your web contact to **publish** the site. |
| Contact form emails not arriving | Check junk. Confirm **Send enquiries to** in Settings. Ask your web contact if it still fails. |
| Need the public site | Click **View site**, or visit hartupconstruction.com.au. |

---

## 13. Good habits

- Log out when you finish, especially on a shared computer.
- Do not share the admin password. Use **Settings** to change it if someone else needs to stop using it.
- Prefer replacing photos rather than stacking many unused uploads.
- If something looks broken after a change, tell your web contact what you clicked and which page you were on.

For technical setup (first-time install, publishing files to the server), see the separate technical notes — not this manual.
