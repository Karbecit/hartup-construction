About Hartup Construction

Our Story

Founded on Integrity, Built on Experience

Hartup Construction was founded in 2009 with a simple mission: to deliver exceptional building projects that stand the test of time. What started as a small residential building operation has grown into a full-service construction company serving clients across New South Wales, Victoria, and Queensland.

Our founder, David Hartup, brought over two decades of on-site experience to the business. Today, our team of 40+ professionals includes licensed builders, project managers, estimators, and specialist tradespeople — all united by a shared commitment to quality.

We believe that great construction is about more than bricks and mortar. It's about understanding our clients' needs, communicating openly throughout the process, and delivering results that exceed expectations.
